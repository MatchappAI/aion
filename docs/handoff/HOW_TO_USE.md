# How to use AION (local)

Start it by double-clicking `Start AION.command`; it opens http://localhost:8765. The same steps are in the app under **Help**.

## Settings: which AI
Claude Code is used first when logged in. Add Codex (`codex login`) or an Anthropic/OpenAI key as a backup; when one hits its usage limit the next takes over for an hour. **Test** sends a tiny request. **Use this** pins one; **Go back to automatic** undoes it.

## Value Creation
1. Leave **Hide customer and staff names from the AI** ticked (default) unless you need names in the prompts.
2. Drop the books: QuickBooks **General Ledger** (24–36 months, all accounts, Excel) is best; **P&L Detail** or a **PDF / CIM** also work.
3. Watch the five steps (under a minute when saved answers exist; a few minutes otherwise).
4. Read the tabs:
   - **Real profit:** reported → real profit → each change → could reach, plus month-by-month trends. Answer the biggest unknown (is that payroll line the owner?). A market salary for whoever runs the firm after closing always comes off profit, whether the seller stays or a leader is hired.
   - **Price & returns:** price against the price guide, a market reference line for the firm's size (labelled as unverified), deal costs, how the firm is bought (assets, shares or F-reorganization), loan, vehicles and returns, including a bad case with lower profit and a lower value.
   - **Plan:** ranked actions, 100 days, "grow to a target multiple" model, and the monthly check-in after you buy. **Build it** gives steps and a copyable prompt.
   - **Questions & checks:** the fit check (PASS/WATCH/FAIL/NEEDS DATA with evidence) and the seller questions, including platform account ownership, partner status, change-of-control consents, commission tails, pass-through ad spend, client tenure, contractors, non-competes and prepaid retainers.
5. **1-page PDF** (needs Chrome) and **Excel** save to Downloads; a corner message confirms.
6. **Make the plan sharper:** add a payroll or sales-by-customer report.
7. Deal tabs across the top: click to switch, double-click to rename, **+ New deal**. **Close this deal** archives it; nothing is deleted. **Run the AI again** ignores saved answers.

## Proprietary Search
1. Pick **Find firms to buy** or **Find brands**. Type a sentence or click an example; optional filters.
2. **Search** (4–8 minutes; two can run at once). Watch the steps or come back later.
3. Results: fit score, timing badge, every fact with its link, verified contacts. **Save / Priority / Reject**, notes, **Deal stage**, **Watch weekly** (runs while the app is open), **Refresh data signals**.
4. Home page: **Owners to contact** (every ecommerce consulting firm found, ranked, filterable by segment, with Write outreach on each row; Outreach shows the same list), deal pipeline, watched searches, history, **your network**, **Brand demand radar** (brands likely to need retail or marketplace help), data sources (upload your contacts CSV to see warm introductions).
