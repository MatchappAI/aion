# Demo script (about 3½ minutes)
The screen explains itself, so you can stay quiet.

## Value Creation (about 90 seconds)
Use a real company's books if you have them. If you use the test files, say so: they are not a real company.

1. **Upload:** drop in the books. Let the run play (under a minute, one step at a time).
2. **Chart:** let them read the headline. Every bar traces back to a real payment.
3. **Biggest unknown:** click Yes, then No. The chart redraws.
4. **Easiest wins:** open one. It has a ready-to-use plan and AI prompt.
5. **Make the plan sharper:** drop in the payroll and sales reports. New bars appear in a second.
5b. **AION fit:** show the thesis check (every row has its evidence; NEEDS DATA where there is none), then record one diligence answer (for example the share of new revenue from RFPs) and watch the tool fit change. Say: the edge is a repeatable operating system plus our own tools, validated deal by deal.
6. **What it would take:** on the Plan tab, click 3×. Change growth or add-ons and the year it is reached moves. Say it is a what-if, not counted.
7. **Good deal for investors:** click Holding company, then PE fund. The returns change with the type of owner.

## Proprietary Search (about 2 minutes)
Everything here is real: real searches, real firms, every fact linked to its source.

8. **Home:** point at the Demand radar (companies likely to need ops leaders before the job is posted; built for talent and operations-advisory firms), the running totals and the Deal pipeline. Say plainly how many owners are in it.
9. **Open an Acquisition Targets search:** read the verdict line at the top.
10. **Open a target:** show the criteria table. Must-haves are marked, and a firm that breaks one (size, niche, payment terms) is capped at 40%, so it can't rank high.
11. **Signals from data sources:** in the same drawer, show what was read directly (domain record, website history, news) and that it sets timing, not fit.
12. **Set its deal stage** if you have spoken to the owner. Each change is dated.

If something breaks: click "Replay the run".
